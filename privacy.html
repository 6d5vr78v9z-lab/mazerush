Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

MazeRush ("the App") is a personal content-creation tool that generates and publishes original short-form videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

1. Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal data from any users. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking.

2. Platform authorization

The App uses OAuth tokens granted by the account owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts. These tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.

3. Third parties

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. Video uploads go directly to the respective platform's official API.
